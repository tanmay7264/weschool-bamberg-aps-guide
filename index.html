Confirm the route before you register or pay

Ask WeSchool and Bamberg to confirm in writing whether this cohort needs Process A group verification, qualifies for an exemption, or must follow another route. The German Missions visa page lists an APS-certificate exemption for non-German-degree studies. APS also publishes Process A for institutional exchanges. These statements concern related but different requirements; neither is permission to assume the cohort route.

If the institutions confirm that no APS procedure is needed, retain their instruction and the relevant exemption evidence and continue with Bamberg admission and the applicable visa process. Stop the APS registration and payment path.

If Process A applies, obtain the group instructions and use this guide. If there is a German degree, a double degree, an uncertain qualification category, an existing APS account, or an earlier refusal, ask for case-specific instructions before registering or paying. Do not create a duplicate account to resolve uncertainty.

You have been selected for a WeSchool exchange at the University of Bamberg. Start by confirming with your international office whether APS group verification is required for your cohort. If Process A is confirmed, you register individually, while the universities arrange the group and coordinate payment and submission.

This guide covers a short-term exchange in which you remain enrolled at your home institution and do not receive a German degree. APS verifies academic records; it does not grant university admission or issue a visa. Your WeSchool selection, Bamberg admission and visa application are separate milestones.

Use the numbered journey in order. Documents can be gathered while the institutions arrange the group. Follow the confirmed cohort instructions at registration and payment checkpoints. Mark a step complete only after you have the evidence described in that step.

STEP 01 · Student

Keep your selection record

Turn your selection into a clear set of next actions and a record you can refer to throughout the exchange.

What you do

  1. Save the WeSchool selection or nomination email. Note the host university, intended exchange semester, approximate stay and the programme in which you remain enrolled.
  2. Identify the WeSchool international office contact and ask who coordinates the APS group. Record an official email address, the student handover deadline and how the office wants documents delivered.
  3. Create a private exchange folder with separate areas for identity records, education records, APS registration, payment evidence and university correspondence. Keep originals secure and prepare working copies.
  4. Check that your passport details are available and accurate. If a passport is missing, expiring or being renewed, tell the coordinator early and ask how that affects nomination and APS registration.

What to check

Selection is not Bamberg admission. Do not describe yourself as admitted until Bamberg issues its admission letter. Do not invent travel dates or use an old cohort deadline.

If something is missing or unclear

If the host, semester or programme is incorrect, resolve it with WeSchool before entering the details into any application.

Ready to move on when

You have the selection record, the correct coordinator and a list of the next university deadlines.

Sources: S7 Bamberg exchange application instructions, S8 German Missions study visa guidance

STEP 02 · WeSchool + Bamberg

Confirm whether APS Process A applies

Establish the correct procedure before you create a registration or incur a fee.

What you do

  1. Ask the coordinator to confirm that you are part of a recognised institutional exchange, remain enrolled at WeSchool and will not receive a German degree. The published Process A leaflet describes bachelor or master enrolment at the Indian partner institution.
  2. For a PGDM or another diploma, ask WeSchool and Bamberg to confirm that the qualification and cohort are accepted for Process A and how the qualification must be entered on the APS form. Write the actual award title; do not relabel a diploma as a master's degree.
  3. Ask whether the non-German-degree visa certificate exemption or a qualifying publicly funded scholarship applies to you. The Process A leaflet describes European publicly funded awards meeting the current monthly BAfoeG threshold. Ask the offices to confirm the current applicable conditions for your award; having any scholarship is not enough.
  4. If you already registered, paid or received an APS result, give the coordinator the existing reference privately and request instructions. The leaflet excludes candidates previously refused in the individual procedure because of fraudulent documents.

What to check

A dMAT exemption for an officially confirmed exchange does not mean exemption from APS group verification. TestAS and university or visa language requirements must also be distinguished from the APS process.

If something is missing or unclear

Exemption confirmed: retain proof and move to the admission and visa handover. Route unclear: continue gathering documents, but pause registration and payment.

Ready to move on when

The universities have confirmed the route and explained any exemption, PGDM entry or existing-account issue that applies to you.

Sources: S1 APS Process A leaflet, S6 APS dMAT requirements and exemptions, S8 German Missions study visa guidance

STEP 03 · Student + WeSchool + Bamberg

Complete the Bamberg exchange application

Keep the university application moving alongside APS preparation.

What you do

  1. WeSchool selects and nominates candidates. After nomination, watch for the application link emailed by Bamberg's International Office; check spam as well as your inbox.
  2. Open the university link and follow the current HISinOne exchange application instructions. Prepare the requested forms and photograph and submit them by the deadline given for your semester.
  3. Review your name and passport details against your identity documents. Check that you selected the exchange semester and programme corresponding to your nomination.
  4. Monitor the application platform and your registered email for missing items. After review, download and retain the official Bamberg admission letter from the platform.

What to check

The public Bamberg page currently lists 15 May for winter-semester applications and 1 December for summer-semester applications. Recheck it and your invitation for the relevant year; WeSchool may set an earlier internal deadline.

If something is missing or unclear

If no invitation arrives after nomination, ask WeSchool to confirm nomination and contact Bamberg using its official exchange channels. Do not create an unrelated degree application.

Ready to move on when

You have submitted the exchange application and recorded its status. Retain the admission letter when issued.

Sources: S7 Bamberg exchange application instructions

STEP 04 · WeSchool + Bamberg + APS

Obtain the institutional group instructions

Ensure your individual registration can be linked to the correct university group.

What you do

  1. The Indian or German institution contacts APS at groups@aps-india.de with the cooperation agreement details, selected candidate list and representatives' contact information. APS assigns the group number to the institutions.
  2. Ask the coordinator to share the confirmed group number, registration instructions, payment arrangement, document handover deadline and the office responsible for collective dispatch.
  3. Confirm that the group list uses the same name, date of birth and student details as your documents. Give corrections to the coordinator before the group packet is finalised.
  4. Gather documents while the group is being arranged. Treat coordinator confirmation as the practical checkpoint before registration/payment; the public form does not establish that a group number is a technical prerequisite for creating an account.

What to check

Do not invent a group number or enter it into an unrelated field. The leaflet requires it on the group envelope and transfer reference. A single application without an assigned group number cannot be allocated to the group.

If something is missing or unclear

If group instructions are pending, keep preparing documents. The coordinator should resolve the group arrangement with APS; do not independently courier an unallocated application.

Ready to move on when

You know your group reference and have the cohort's written instructions for registration, payment and document collection.

Sources: S1 APS Process A leaflet

STEP 05 · Student

Gather the required documents

Build a complete packet using the exchange checklist rather than the individual graduate checklist.

What you do

  1. Open the APS checklists page and download Process A and the current Student Authorisation Letter. Check that your cohort has not received newer written instructions from APS.
  2. Collect the common documents in the document checklist. Obtain the original enrolment statement from WeSchool and ask the coordinator which academic category applies to your PGDM.
  3. For a confirmed postgraduate/master category, prepare your bachelor's certificate, every bachelor semester marksheet and all available current university marksheets. For a confirmed bachelor category, prepare the Class XII certificate and all bachelor marksheets to date.
  4. Keep your Class X and XII details available for the public form even where a particular certificate is not listed in the Process A postgraduate packet. Distinguish information needed to fill a field from documents required in the submitted packet.
  5. The signed application and payment receipt become available later. Track them as pending items rather than declaring the packet complete prematurely.

What to check

Send copies of academic degrees, diplomas, certificates and marksheets. The original enrolment statement is specifically required by the Process A list and is an exception to the academic-originals warning.

If something is missing or unclear

If a certificate, semester result or enrolment statement is unavailable, ask the coordinator what APS will accept. Do not substitute a different document or a self-written declaration without confirmation.

Ready to move on when

All preparatory documents are available, with remaining post-registration/payment items clearly identified.

Sources: S1 APS Process A leaflet, S2 APS checklists, S5 Student Authorisation Letter

STEP 06 · Student + WeSchool

Check every document against the form

Catch discrepancies before submission so the information on paper and online agrees.

What you do

  1. Compare your passport name, date and place of birth, Aadhaar and academic records. Flag spelling differences, initials, changed names or an updated passport to the coordinator; do not edit official scans to make them match.
  2. Check that copies are legible, in colour and A4 size, with both sides where relevant. Keep seals, signatures, grades, certificate numbers and page edges visible.
  3. If a record is only in an Indian regional language, arrange a notarised English translation as required by APS. English or bilingual records generally do not need notarisation solely for APS.
  4. Arrange semester records in chronological order and verify that each completed semester is represented. Identify the awarding university separately from the college or institute.
  5. Use recorded percentages and CGPA. If conversion is required, obtain the institution's official rule; do not use an internet conversion formula or manufacture a passing year for an unfinished programme.

What to check

Do not upload or circulate credentials, identity documents or the group's personal records in public class chats. Use the institution's designated channel for document handover.

If something is missing or unclear

Missing or inconsistent record: correct it through the issuing institution or obtain coordinator/APS instructions, then repeat the check.

Ready to move on when

Your documents are readable, your details agree, and unresolved discrepancies have a documented next action.

Sources: S1 APS Process A leaflet, S4 APS frequently asked questions

STEP 07 · Student

Create your APS account

Create your personal registration using the official public APS form and the confirmed group instructions.

What you do

  1. Start at https://aps-india.de and choose the application/register link. The public application page checked for this guide is https://aps-india.de/aps-process/. Read its information and acknowledgement before continuing.
  2. Keep your documents and coordinator instructions beside you. Use a working email account you will continue to access during the exchange. Enter it twice carefully and check for spelling mistakes.
  3. Choose a username and a strong, unique password. The current public form specifies a minimum of seven characters. Store your own credentials privately.
  4. Prepare the required photograph upload. The public form currently requests JPG or JPEG up to 30 KB. Resize a copy, check that it remains clear and retain the original photograph.
  5. Use the form-field guide for personal and education sections. Inspect each screen as you go. The portal may change; if a required field cannot be answered truthfully, obtain clarification instead of entering filler data.

What to check

The group leaflet tells students to register like individual applicants; it does not establish a separate Exchange button or group-number input on the public form. Do not look for invented controls. Resolve payment handling with the coordinator before final submission.

If something is missing or unclear

Technical error or no usable confirmation: retain the error text, date and reference if available, then contact the coordinator/APS. Do not repeatedly create accounts or pay again.

Ready to move on when

Your registration details have been reviewed and you have retained any reference or confirmation actually provided by the portal.

Sources: S1 APS Process A leaflet, S3 APS public application form, S9 APS contact form

STEP 08 · Student

Complete education and other form sections

Represent your actual academic history accurately, including completed degrees and current study.

What you do

  1. Enter Class X and XII details from the relevant board records. Where a centre number is requested, use the correct examination record. If a detail does not exist, ask before entering a guessed number.
  2. For bachelor's education, distinguish the university awarding the degree from the college attended. Enter the exact course, duration, specialisation, completion status and recorded result.
  3. Enter current postgraduate/diploma study using the classification confirmed for your cohort. The form has separate master's and additional degree/diploma areas. A PGDM must retain its actual title; the applicable section requires confirmation.
  4. Complete test and language fields only as applicable to your actual qualifications and current official requirements. The form displaying a test section does not establish that you must sit that test.
  5. Read the education-verification fields carefully. The public form includes academic portal login and professor-email fields. Any requested credentials belong only in the authentic APS channel if applicable; this guide does not collect them.

What to check

Officially confirmed exchange programmes are exempt from dMAT under the current APS guidance. APS currently says TestAS is not mandatory for its verification. University and visa language/test conditions are separate; confirm those independently.

If something is missing or unclear

If a mandatory field forces an inaccurate answer, stop at that point and seek APS instructions through the coordinator. Do not choose a false qualification or fabricated score to proceed.

Ready to move on when

Every applicable section reflects the source records, and the coordinator has resolved any PGDM or mandatory-field ambiguity.

Sources: S3 APS public application form, S4 APS frequently asked questions, S6 APS dMAT requirements and exemptions

STEP 09 · Student

Review print and personally sign

Make the signed paper record consistent with the online registration.

What you do

  1. Before finalising, compare the completed application with your passport and academic records. Recheck email, contact number, institution names, status, marks and course details.
  2. Follow the coordinator-confirmed group instructions for completing the registration/payment interface. Retain the actual registration confirmation, completed application and any available application reference. Do not assume a draft has been submitted.
  3. Print the completed application and personally sign wherever required. The Process A leaflet also requires a personally signed authorisation form.
  4. Download the current Student Authorisation Letter from the checklist page. Fill your name, birth date, date and place and sign it. Read every declaration, including those about recognition and authenticity; ask for clarification if you cannot truthfully make a declaration.
  5. Keep a copy of the exact signed set you hand over. Check that no signature page is missing and that printed pages match the final submitted details.

What to check

Do not sign blank forms, ask someone else to sign for you or assume an unsigned printout is sufficient. Post-login print/download controls must be followed as actually presented by the portal.

If something is missing or unclear

Error discovered after submission: notify the coordinator/APS with your reference and ask how to correct it. Do not silently change only the printed copy or register again.

Ready to move on when

You hold the completed, personally signed application and authorisation letter, with a retained copy.

Sources: S1 APS Process A leaflet, S3 APS public application form, S5 Student Authorisation Letter

STEP 10 · WeSchool or designated group payer

Complete the coordinated group payment

Make one correctly referenced group payment and keep evidence for every application.

What you do

  1. The Process A leaflet currently states INR 18,000 per applicant and requires the group fees to be transferred collectively in one amount, quoting the APS group number. Processing starts once the collective fee is credited.
  2. Ask the coordinator who collects student contributions, who makes the collective APS transfer, and what evidence each student receives. Reconfirm the current fee and bank details against the official leaflet and any newer APS instructions.
  3. The general portal recommends CCAvenue and displays individual payment fields. This differs from the group leaflet. Follow the specific group instructions confirmed by APS through the institutions; do not independently choose online payment or invent offline-payment entries.
  4. If you contribute to the institution, retain that receipt. Also obtain the relevant copy of the actual collective APS transfer receipt for your packet; a student contribution receipt alone is not proof that APS received the group payment.
  5. Match the paid student count to the final group list. For illustration only, five students at INR 18,000 each total INR 90,000; use your actual confirmed group size and current fee.

What to check

APS fees are non-refundable. Do not pay twice because a portal status has not updated. No account number is reproduced here: the payer should use the live official source and confirmed institutional instructions.

If something is missing or unclear

Payment failed, deducted without confirmation, or form demands an unconfirmed individual payment: pause and raise the transaction/reference details through the coordinator or APS contact form before any repeat payment.

Ready to move on when

The designated payer has retained the collective transfer evidence, the group reference is correct and the coordinator has confirmed the next submission action.

Sources: S1 APS Process A leaflet, S3 APS public application form, S4 APS frequently asked questions

STEP 11 · Student + WeSchool or Bamberg

Hand over the packet for collective submission

Submit a complete, traceable group application through the responsible institution.

What you do

  1. Use the final handover checklist: signed application, signed authorisation, photograph, identity copies, required academic copies, original enrolment statement, coordinator details and collective fee receipt.
  2. Order each student's packet consistently and label it as instructed by the coordinator. Ask for an acknowledgement of handover and keep a scan of your own completed packet.
  3. The institution checks the full candidate list, cooperation documents, current representative contacts and fee evidence. The Process A leaflet requires all group applications to be submitted together.
  4. The responsible office marks the group envelope clearly with the APS-assigned group number, checks the current APS mailing address and arranges post/courier. Students should not independently send a separate unallocated packet.
  5. Retain the courier receipt, tracking number and delivery record at group level. Courier delivery confirms delivery of a packet, not completeness, payment reconciliation or successful verification.

What to check

The FAQ lists: Academic Evaluation Centre India, Gate No. 3, DLTA Complex, R.K. Khanna Stadium, 1 Africa Avenue, 110029 New Delhi, India. Recheck before dispatch. Personal drop-offs and unsolicited emailed packets do not replace the published submission process.

If something is missing or unclear

Packet incomplete: return to document collection/review. Delivery delayed or returned: the dispatching office follows up with the courier and checks address/instructions before resending.

Ready to move on when

Your handover is acknowledged and the coordinator has retained the group dispatch and delivery evidence.

Sources: S1 APS Process A leaflet, S4 APS frequently asked questions

STEP 12 · APS + Bamberg + Student

Follow verification and hand over to visa preparation

Respond to verification requests and obtain confirmation of the group outcome through the correct channel.

What you do

  1. Monitor your registered email and coordinate with the international office. If APS requests additional records, provide the exact requested items through the specified channel and retain the request and response.
  2. If an institution has not responded to verification, ask its examinations/records office to respond through an official institutional channel. APS says private email confirmations cannot replace official institutional verification.
  3. Treat the university coordinator as the primary group contact. An account status or courier delivery alone is not proof of a successful Process A outcome. There is no guaranteed group turnaround date in the leaflet.
  4. For Process A, APS emails the result to the concerned German university; applicants do not receive an individual APS certificate. Ask Bamberg to confirm your outcome and tell you what group verification or exemption evidence to use for the visa process.
  5. Retain the admission letter, verified cohort instructions, relevant result/exemption confirmation and your references. Open the current German Missions visa guidance and follow the route for your circumstances. Admission, finance, insurance and other visa requirements must be checked separately.

What to check

Do not wait for an individual digital APS PDF on the group route. APS verification does not guarantee a visa. Process A is tied to the exchange; changes to the German university/programme require the applicable institutional and APS instructions.

If something is missing or unclear

Additional documents requested: return to collection and review, then respond. No result near your deadline: escalate through the coordinator with references and dates; avoid repeated duplicate enquiries.

Ready to move on when

Bamberg has confirmed the relevant result or the universities have confirmed the exemption path, and you know what evidence to use for visa preparation.

Sources: S1 APS Process A leaflet, S4 APS frequently asked questions, S8 German Missions study visa guidance

The institutional packet

The universities arrange the cooperation agreement, selected candidate list, representative details, collective payment and group dispatch. Give your own correct details to the designated coordinator. The group packet must be submitted together.

Understand the APS form

Prepare these details before opening the official application. This guide does not collect them or submit an application.

Create Account

Fields: Create Username; Password; Enter password again; Upload Image

Use a unique username and password. The current form shows a minimum password length of seven characters and a JPG/JPEG photograph upload up to 30 KB. Make a compressed copy of the photograph; confirm that it stays readable.

Check before continuing. Do not save credentials in this guide or share them with classmates. Portal requirements may change.

Personal Details

Fields: Firstname; Lastname; Email and confirmation; Gender; Nationality; Date and Place of Birth; Indian citizenship; Aadhaar; Passport; contact numbers; address

Use your passport as the reference for your legal name and birth details. Enter the working email twice. Copy identity numbers directly from records and check digit by digit. Use an address and contact number at which you can reliably receive communication.

Check before continuing. For a single name, a changed name, an unusual passport format or an unresolved Aadhaar mismatch, obtain APS instructions rather than improvising a name split or placeholder number.

Education Details I

Fields: Class 10 board, year, centre number, percentage; Class 12 board, year, centre number, percentage; JEE scores where applicable

Copy the board's name and recorded results from your documents. Class X board, passing year and percentage are visibly required on the public form. Keep the underlying records ready even when they are not in your Process A academic packet.

Check before continuing. A form field is not a new instruction to take JEE or supply an unrelated document. For grades without a percentage or missing centre details, follow the issuing institution's rule and APS guidance.

Education Details II

Check before continuing. Do not describe an unfinished programme as completed, guess a passing year or rename PGDM to MBA/Master. If the portal will not accept a truthful entry, ask APS through the coordinator.
